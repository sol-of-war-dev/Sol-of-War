Privacy Policy

Sol of War · Effective October 31, 2026

About this policy

Sol of War inc., a California corporation, operates the game and website at solofwar.fun. This policy covers accounts, gameplay, wallets, payments, the cosmetic shop and support. Contact team@solofwar.fun about privacy or your information.

Information used by the service

Account information includes your account identifier, username, public wallet addresses, authentication records and profile settings. We process match participation, game actions and replays, results, ratings, progression, payment requests and transaction signatures. Shop records include purchases, token burns, cosmetic entitlements, equipped items and images you upload. Support messages include information you choose to provide. Hosting, authentication and security services may process IP addresses, browser details and request logs.

Why information is used

We use this information to authenticate accounts, run matches, verify results, process payments and purchases, display profiles and cosmetics, restore service, investigate abuse and respond to support or legal requests. Cookies and browser storage used by the site and authentication provider support sign-in, preferences and pending transaction recovery; disabling them can affect those features.

Public profiles, uploads and blockchain records

Your username, public profile information and gameplay can be visible to other players and spectators. Profile visibility settings control the supported statistics shown on your profile. Equipped custom cosmetics can be displayed to other players; do not upload private or sensitive images. Solana addresses, transfers and token burns are public blockchain records. Linking a wallet to activity can make that activity identifiable. We cannot delete or make public blockchain records private.

Wallets and private keys

New user-owned wallets use Privy. You approve spending through your wallet; Sol of War registers no server spending signer for those personal wallets. Private-key export uses Privy’s protected interface. Never send a private key or recovery phrase to support. Legacy platform-managed wallets and match pools have separate custody arrangements described in the terms; their signing material and backups remain sensitive operational records.

Providers and disclosures

Privy provides authentication and wallet infrastructure. Railway provides application hosting and storage. Solana RPC services process blockchain requests, and wallet providers process the actions you approve. These providers receive information needed for their services and have their own privacy terms. Authorized operators can access records needed for support, payment reconciliation and security. Information may also be disclosed where legally required or to investigate abuse and protect users. Linked services, including pump.fun, operate independently under their own policies.

Retention, security and international processing

Account, game, payment, upload and security records may be retained for service operation, reconciliation, disputes, fraud prevention and applicable legal obligations. Deletion requests are reviewed against those needs; backup copies may persist until their retention cycle ends. Providers may process information in other countries. Access controls and other safeguards reduce risk but cannot guarantee security.

Your requests and choices

Email team@solofwar.fun to request access, correction, export or deletion of your account information, or to ask about retention. We may need to verify account ownership without requesting your private key. We will explain any limits, including records needed for unsettled payments or legal obligations. Applicable privacy rights depend on your location. Disconnecting a wallet or signing out does not itself delete stored records.

Children and changes

Paid play is restricted to adults legally eligible to participate under the terms. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information that should be removed. We update this policy as practices change and revise its effective date; additional notice or consent will be provided where required.
